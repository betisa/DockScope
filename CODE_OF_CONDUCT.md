# Code of Conduct

DockScope contributors are expected to keep project interactions professional, constructive, and focused on the work.

## Expected behavior

- Treat contributors and users with respect.
- Critique code and ideas rather than people.
- Provide actionable technical feedback.
- Assume good faith while still enforcing security and quality standards.
- Keep issue and review discussions relevant to the repository.
- Respect privacy and do not publish credentials, private logs, personal data, or security-sensitive information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, deliberate disruption, doxxing, credential disclosure, or repeated bad-faith behavior are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close or lock discussions, reject contributions, or restrict participation when behavior materially harms the project or its contributors.

For sensitive conduct concerns, contact the repository maintainer privately rather than opening a public issue.
